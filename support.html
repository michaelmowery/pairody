Memory match, played by ear.

Need help or have an idea for a board? Email mountmtech@gmail.com. Tell us your device and the board you were playing, and we'll get back to you.

What is Pairody?

The classic memory match game, where every card is a sound. Flip a pad, listen, then find the pad that goes with it: the same animal call, a joke and its punchline, a country and its capital, and many more.

I can't hear anything

Turn up the volume. On the website on an iPhone, flip off silent mode too; the App Store app plays even on silent. Headphones help on the trickier boards.

How do I change boards?

Tap the board name at the top to see every board, grouped into Sounds, Music, Words and Learning. Tap How to play under the board name to read how the one you picked works.

Too easy, or too hard?

Pick Small, Classic or Big boards. Sound names show on each pad while it plays; turn on Difficult mode for sounds only.

Not sure how it works?

Tap Watch a demo in How to play and Pairody plays a board by itself.

Where are my best scores?

Best scores are saved on your device for each board and size. There are no accounts, so they don't move between devices, and deleting the app or clearing browser data removes them.

Privacy

Pairody has no accounts and no ads. It sends anonymous usage counts that you can turn off under the i button. Read the privacy policy.
