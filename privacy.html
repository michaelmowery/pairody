Privacy policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

Pairody is a memory game played by ear, made by Mount M Technologies, LLC. This policy covers the website at pairody.app and the Pairody apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. Pairody is built to know as little about you as possible.

No accounts, no ads

Pairody has no sign-up, no accounts and no advertising. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or anything else about you.

Scores and settings stay on your device

Your best scores and preferences (like game size, Timed and which notes you've seen) are saved in local storage on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app or clearing your browser data removes them.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which boards and features people use, Pairody sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. A count looks like "the Animals board was picked" or "a game was cleared". Each one includes:

  • the name of the action or page,
  • the page that linked you here, if any (the app never sends one),
  • your screen size, and your browser or device type and operating system (from the standard browser header),
  • an approximate country or region, worked out from your IP address. GoatCounter doesn't store the IP address itself.

GoatCounter uses no cookies and builds no profile of you. It tells repeat visits apart with a short-lived hash that is regularly thrown away, so it can't follow you over time or across other websites or apps. The counts are never sold, never used for advertising, and never combined with other data. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

You can switch this off at any time: tap the i button, then turn off Anonymous usage counts under Privacy. Nothing is sent while you're offline.

Your music (website beta)

On the website, the Your music beta plays songs through YouTube's own embedded player, so YouTube's privacy policy applies while you use it. Pairody never sends your playlists or song links anywhere. Your music isn't part of the apps.

Children

Pairody doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
