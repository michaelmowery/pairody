Tips

  • Playlists must be public or unlisted. Liked Music and private playlists won't load, so copy songs into an unlisted playlist.
  • Some official music videos block playback outside YouTube. Pairody swaps those out when it has spare songs.
  • Easy mode shows each song's title and artist on the pad while its clip plays, like the now-playing card in YouTube Music.
  • On iPhone, if the first pad is silent, tap it again.
  • Videos play through YouTube's own player and stay on screen while they play.
  • Your best score is saved on this device for each playlist (or set of song links), with Hard and Easy mode boards kept separately.
