# Sound credits

The Animals deck plays real recordings from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) and [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/). Every clip is **public domain or CC0**, so Pairody can use them freely, including in any paid version, with no attribution required. We credit the recordists anyway.

Each clip was trimmed to one call, mixed to mono, peak-normalized and encoded as a 64 kbps MP3. If a clip fails to load, the game plays its synthesized sound instead.
